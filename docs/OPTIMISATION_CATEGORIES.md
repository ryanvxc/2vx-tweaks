# Optimisation categories

How the 320 tweaks are organised after the 0.5 audit ([VISUALS_SECTION_AUDIT.md](VISUALS_SECTION_AUDIT.md)). Every tweak has one primary location.

## Structure

There's no separate page for these. A tweak's `category` picks its page and tab (`CATEGORY_TAB` in `src/lib/tweak-presentation.ts`) and its `section` picks the section on that tab. Windows and Performance go to Tweaks › Core; Privacy, Gaming, Apps and Power to the Tweaks tab of that name; CPU, GPU, Memory and Storage to Hardware; Network to Network › Tweaks; Services to PC Cleaner › Services. Visuals go to the Visuals tab their section belongs to (`VISUALS_SECTION_TAB`). A short hand-placed set comes first (`TWEAK_SECTIONS`). Search covers every tab of its page.
